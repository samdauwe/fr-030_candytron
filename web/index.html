fr-030: Candytron

The 2003 demo by Farbrausch (Breakpoint 2003, 2nd place): chaos, ryg, gizmo and kb. It is made on your machine: the textures, the models and the music are generated from the production's 120 KB of data, as it was in 2003. Three minutes with sound; it needs WebGL 2.
